## Explicit boundaries

- Aika as general time versus appointment are separate senses; ajan is a nominal appointment object only in its reviewed example, not a claim about the verb ajaa in general.
- Voi in a positive ability phrase differs from the negative wellbeing phrase En voi hyvin. In en voi the form is connegative; the noun meaning butter is not reviewed.
- Pitää refers to necessity in these examples, not holding or liking. Tuli is a past-tense form of tulla in these examples, not the fire noun.
- One authored legacy item has lemma tuli though its example uses the verb tulla. The original item is preserved and its exception is explicit.
- No learner mastery or cross-SRS review transfer occurs. Only documented item meanings are linked.
