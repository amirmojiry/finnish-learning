# Lesson learning objectives contract (P1)

The optional `learning_objectives` property separates communicative, lexical and grammar goals. Existing lesson records remain valid without it. It is checked after deterministic activity injection by `validateImplementedPath` and can also be checked directly with `validateLessonObjectives`.

- `communicative`: `{id, can_do_fa}`, a Persian can-do statement.
- `lexical`: `{target_id, status}`, with a section item ID and `introduced` or `reviewed`.
- `grammar`: `{concept_id, status, prerequisite_concept_ids, explanation_fa, examples, evidence}`. Grammar concept IDs and prerequisite lists must match `curriculum.grammar_concepts` derived from the master curriculum. Examples require reviewed Finnish and Persian text.
- `evidence`: `{activity_type, item_id, mode}` identifies a positively graded activity after runtime injection. Alternatively, `activity_index` may select an activity. Each selected activity must explicitly declare `grammar_concept_ids` containing the objective's concept ID. `mode` is `recognition`, `supported_production`, or `independent_production`. Independent production rejects explicit scaffolding. This validates an authored assessment opportunity, not learner proficiency.

Current mapped simple-past examples: A2.1 Section 1 Lesson 1 **introduces** `a21-imperfect` through positively assessed `kävit` production; Section 1 Lessons 2–10 **review** the concept using distinct graded forms (`Menin`, `Ostin`, `lähti`, `Varasin`, `oli`, `Kävin`, `ostin`, `kävin`, `Näin`). Section 2 Lesson 3 reviews `alkoivat`. Lessons 4–6 retain their fuller existing `grammar_fa` notes for case and negative-past context, but those additional structures are **not** claimed as independently assessed grammar objectives. `ajoissa` and `myöhässä` are lexical review forms already authored in A1.2; `lippua` was also positively assessed in A1.2 Section 2 Lesson 6 and is therefore reviewed here, not newly introduced. These forms do not receive fabricated lexeme/sense identifiers. Section 1 Lesson 10 is a cumulative checkpoint with fifteen lexical objectives all marked reviewed and no new targets. Sequencing, object cases and verb rections mentioned in prose are not credited as separate assessed grammar concepts. Section 2 lessons 1–2 now distinguish assessed polite requests (`a12-requests`), necessity (`a13-necessity`) and everyday negation (`a12-negation-expand`) through contextual production/transform tasks. Full conditional morphology is not introduced, and a guided exercise does not prove independent fluency. Other sections and grammar concepts stay unmapped until a separate evidence-linked PR. Grammar prose, distractors and structure names alone do not prove taught or mastered grammar.

This change does not alter lesson progression or user storage. Lesson-overview UI, activity-count flexibility and additional lesson mappings are separate follow-up work.

A2.1 Section 2 lessons 3–4 now include context-specific positively graded recognition of `vähän kuumetta`, supported symptom-past reporting, controlled polite clarification requests, and supported `pitää + tehdä` necessity. These are linked to reviewed `a12-quantities`, `a21-imperfect`, `a12-requests`, and `a13-necessity` targets. They do not assess unconstrained partitive paradigms, conditional morphology or medical competency.

A2.1 Section 2 lessons 5–10 now distinguish reviewed, positively assessed supported service requests, location questions, present morphology and necessity frames. Vocabulary already authored in earlier lessons is marked reviewed; checkpoint lessons contain no new vocabulary. These guided activities do not certify free production or general tense/case mastery.

All 20 lessons were inspected; mapping all 20 to *some* grammar concept is not evidence that all A2.1 structures are covered or mastered. Remaining instructional work includes broader plural partitive, -maan forms, independent connected narratives and direct assessment of untested lexical target forms. Keep these gaps open for R2/P3 exercise-length improvements.
