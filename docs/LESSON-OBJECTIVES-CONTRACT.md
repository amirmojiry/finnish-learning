# Lesson learning objectives contract (P1)

The optional `learning_objectives` property separates communicative, lexical and grammar goals. Existing lesson records remain valid without it. It is checked after deterministic activity injection by `validateImplementedPath` and can also be checked directly with `validateLessonObjectives`.

- `communicative`: `{id, can_do_fa}`, a Persian can-do statement.
- `lexical`: `{target_id, status}`, with a section item ID and `introduced` or `reviewed`.
- `grammar`: `{concept_id, status, prerequisite_concept_ids, explanation_fa, examples, evidence}`. Grammar concept IDs and prerequisite lists must match `curriculum.grammar_concepts` derived from the master curriculum. Examples require reviewed Finnish and Persian text.
- `evidence`: `{activity_type, item_id, mode}` identifies a positively graded activity after runtime injection. Alternatively, `activity_index` may select an activity. Each selected activity must explicitly declare `grammar_concept_ids` containing the objective's concept ID. `mode` is `recognition`, `supported_production`, or `independent_production`. Independent production rejects explicit scaffolding. This validates an authored assessment opportunity, not learner proficiency.

Current mapped simple-past examples: A2.1 Section 1 Lesson 1 **introduces** `a21-imperfect` through positively assessed `kävit` production; Section 1 Lessons 2–10 **review** the concept using distinct graded forms (`Menin`, `Ostin`, `lähti`, `Varasin`, `oli`, `Kävin`, `ostin`, `kävin`, `Näin`). Section 2 Lesson 3 reviews `alkoivat`. The original grammar notes remain contextual; only specifically graded case decisions are assessed and negative-past proficiency is not claimed. `ajoissa` and `myöhässä` are lexical review forms already authored in A1.2; `lippua` was also positively assessed in A1.2 Section 2 Lesson 6 and is therefore reviewed here, not newly introduced. These forms do not receive fabricated lexeme/sense identifiers. Section 1 Lesson 10 is a cumulative checkpoint with fifteen lexical objectives all marked reviewed and no new targets. Selected sequencing, object-case and verb-rection goals now have direct positive grading, with recognition or supported production only; other prose-only patterns remain unassessed. Section 2 lessons 1–2 now distinguish assessed polite requests (`a12-requests`), necessity (`a13-necessity`) and everyday negation (`a12-negation-expand`) through contextual production/transform tasks. Full conditional morphology is not introduced, and a guided exercise does not prove independent fluency. Other sections and grammar concepts stay unmapped until a separate evidence-linked PR. Grammar prose, distractors and structure names alone do not prove taught or mastered grammar.

This change does not alter lesson progression or user storage. Lesson-overview UI, activity-count flexibility and additional lesson mappings are separate follow-up work.

A2.1 Section 2 lessons 3–4 now include context-specific positively graded recognition of `vähän kuumetta`, supported symptom-past reporting, controlled polite clarification requests, and supported `pitää + tehdä` necessity. These are linked to reviewed `a12-quantities`, `a21-imperfect`, `a12-requests`, and `a13-necessity` targets. They do not assess unconstrained partitive paradigms, conditional morphology or medical competency.

Keep these gaps open for R2/P3 exercise-length improvements.

Activity count contract (R2): existing lessons use 15 by default; author a positive integer `activity_count` on a lesson to explicitly change it, or add valid activities to an authored list without an override. Generated lessons default to section `activity_count_per_lesson`, and checkpoint generation requires matching nonempty `checkpoint_targets` and `checkpoint_modes`. Required listening, production and structured/morphology outcomes still apply. Progress scoring uses actual graded attempts and old scores are retained.
