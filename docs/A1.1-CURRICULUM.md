# Complete A1.1 curriculum

[نسخه فارسی](A1.1-CURRICULUM.fa.md)

This document is the implementation specification for the complete A1.1 stage. The machine-readable source is [`data/course/a1.1-curriculum.json`](../data/course/a1.1-curriculum.json).

## Structure

- **4 sections**
- **10 lessons per section**
- **40 lessons total**

## Lesson matrix

### 1. First contact and survival Finnish

Handle a few very common social exchanges with memorized words, short questions and supported responses.

| Lesson | Can-do outcome | High-frequency stream | Topic stream | Expressions / frames | Grammar / morphology | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a1.1-s1-l01 — Greetings | Greet and say goodbye in a few routine situations. | on, ja | hei, moi | Hei!, Moi!, Näkemiin! | fixed greeting formulas | Listening, Speaking |
| a1.1-s1-l02 — My name | Say your name and ask another person's name. | minä, sinä, olen | nimi | Minun nimeni on …, Mikä sinun nimesi on? | olla: minä olen / sinä olet | Listening, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s1-l03 — How are you? | Ask how someone is and give a short conventional answer. | mitä, hyvä | hyvää | Mitä kuuluu?, Hyvää, kiitos., Ihan hyvää. | formulaic partitive in Mitä kuuluu? / hyvää | Listening, Speaking |
| a1.1-s1-l04 — Yes, no and simple negation | Answer yes/no and say that someone or something is not something. | kyllä, ei, ole | vastaus | Kyllä., Ei., Ei ole. | negation with ei + ole as a chunk | Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s1-l05 — Who and what? | Ask who a person is and what a familiar object is. | mikä, hän, tämä | ihminen, esine, kuka | Kuka hän on?, Mikä tämä on? | question words kuka / mikä; tämä + on | Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s1-l06 — Polite basics | Thank, apologize and use a basic polite response. | — | anteeksi, kiitos | Kiitos., Anteeksi., Ole hyvä. | imperative-like formula ole hyvä as a fixed expression | Listening, Speaking |
| a1.1-s1-l07 — I do not understand | Signal lack of understanding and ask for repetition or slower speech. | en | uudestaan, hitaasti | En ymmärrä., Uudestaan, kiitos., Hitaammin, kiitos. | 1st-person negation as a memorized frame | Listening, Speaking |
| a1.1-s1-l08 — This and that | Identify a few familiar objects using this/that. | tämä, se, on | kirja, puhelin, ovi | Tämä on …, Se on … | demonstratives tämä / se + olla | Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s1-l09 — First mini-dialogues | Combine greetings, name, wellbeing and repair phrases in short exchanges. | — | — | Hei! Minun nimeni on …, Mitä kuuluu?, En ymmärrä. Uudestaan, kiitos. | recycling only | Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s1-l10 — Section 1 checkpoint | Handle a supported first-contact interaction using the section's core language. | — | — | — | checkpoint: greetings, olla, question words, simple negation | Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing |

### 2. Me and people around me

Give a small amount of personal information and identify close people using short practiced sentences.

| Lesson | Can-do outcome | High-frequency stream | Topic stream | Expressions / frames | Grammar / morphology | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a1.1-s2-l01 — People and pronouns | Refer to one or more people with basic personal pronouns. | hän, me, he | ystävä | Hän on …, Me olemme …, He ovat … | personal pronouns; olla 3sg/1pl/3pl as chunks | Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s2-l02 — Country and origin | Say where you are from and ask another person. | olen | Suomi, Iran, maa, mistä | Olen Suomesta., Olen Iranista., Mistä olet? | elative -sta/-stä in memorized origin phrases | Listening, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s2-l03 — Languages | Say which language or languages you speak. | — | suomi, persia, englanti, puhun | Puhun suomea., Puhun persiaa., Puhun englantia., Puhun vähän suomea. | language names use explicit partitive forms after puhua: suomea, persiaa, englantia | Listening, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s2-l04 — Where I live | Say where you live and ask where another person lives. | missä | kaupunki, Vaasa, Helsinki, asun | Asun Vaasassa., Missä asut? | inessive -ssa/-ssä in city names as a pattern | Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s2-l05 — My family | Name a few close family members. | — | perhe, äiti, isä, sisar, veli, lapsi | Tämä on minun äitini., Minulla on lapsi. | basic possession as memorized frames | Reading, Speaking |
| a1.1-s2-l06 — Whose? | Express very simple possession with my/your. | minun | nimi, koti, sinun | minun nimeni, sinun nimesi, minun kotini | genitive pronouns minun / sinun; possessive forms as chunks | Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s2-l07 — Age and numbers 0–20 | Say your age and understand numbers from zero to twenty. | yksi, kaksi, kolme | nolla, neljä, viisi, kuusi, seitsemän, kahdeksan, yhdeksän, kymmenen, yksitoista, kaksitoista, kolmetoista, neljätoista, viisitoista, kuusitoista, seitsemäntoista, kahdeksantoista, yhdeksäntoista, kaksikymmentä, ikä | Olen …-vuotias., Kuinka vanha olet? | cardinal numbers 0–20; age frame with -vuotias | Listening, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s2-l08 — Contact details | Understand and give a very short phone number or address with support. | — | puhelinnumero, osoite, katu, numero | Mikä sinun puhelinnumerosi on?, Minun numeroni on … | compound nouns; digit-by-digit production | Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s2-l09 — A short personal profile | Understand and produce a few linked sentences about a person. | — | — | Minun nimeni on …, Olen Suomesta., Olen Iranista., Asun Vaasassa., Asun Helsingissä., Puhun suomea., Puhun persiaa., Puhun englantia. | recycling complete reviewed personal-information frames; do not generate case forms by suffix concatenation | Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s2-l10 — Section 2 checkpoint | Exchange basic personal information and identify close people. | — | — | — | checkpoint: personal information, pronouns, origin, location, family, numbers | Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing |

### 3. Time and a simple day

Understand and produce highly practiced language about days, basic clock times and a simple routine.

| Lesson | Can-do outcome | High-frequency stream | Topic stream | Expressions / frames | Grammar / morphology | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a1.1-s3-l01 — Today and tomorrow | Distinguish today, tomorrow and yesterday in simple statements. | — | eilen, tänään, huomenna | Tänään on …, Huomenna on … | time adverbs as fixed items | Listening, Reading, Speaking |
| a1.1-s3-l02 — Days of the week | Recognize and name the days of the week. | — | maanantai, tiistai, keskiviikko, torstai, perjantai, lauantai, sunnuntai | Tänään on maanantai., Huomenna on tiistai. | weekday nouns; olla in date-like statements | Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s3-l03 — Whole hours | Understand and say simple whole-hour times. | — | aamu, ilta, kello | Kello on yksi., Kello on kaksi. | kello + cardinal number | Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s3-l04 — Parts of the day | Talk about morning, daytime and evening with simple routine phrases. | nyt | aamulla, päivällä, illalla, yöllä | Aamulla minä …, Illalla minä … | adessive time forms as chunks | Listening, Reading, Speaking |
| a1.1-s3-l05 — Common actions: I | Say a few common things you do. | — | syön, juon, nukun, opiskelen, työskentelen, menen, tulen | Minä syön., Minä juon kahvia., Menen kotiin. | present tense 1st person singular; selected verb chunks | Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s3-l06 — Common actions: you | Ask what another person does in a few familiar situations. | mitä | työ, koulu, teet | Mitä teet?, Opiskeletko?, Työskenteletkö? | 2nd person present; yes/no -ko/-kö questions as chunks | Listening, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s3-l07 — I do not … | Negate a few familiar routine verbs. | en | — | En puhu englantia., En työskentele tänään. | 1st person negative: en + connegative form | Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s3-l08 — When? | Ask and answer when a simple event happens. | — | aika, milloin | Milloin?, Tänään., Huomenna., Kello kolme. | question word milloin; short time answers | Listening, Reading, Speaking |
| a1.1-s3-l09 — My simple day | Understand and produce a short sequence describing a routine day. | — | — | Aamulla …, Päivällä …, Illalla … | sequence with time adverbs; recycling present tense | Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s3-l10 — Section 3 checkpoint | Handle a highly supported exchange about day, time and routine. | — | — | — | checkpoint: weekdays, whole hours, routine verbs, negation, when | Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing |

| Lesson | Can-do outcome | High-frequency stream | Topic stream | Expressions / frames | Grammar / morphology | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a1.1-s4-l01 — Home and rooms | Name a few common places in a home. | — | huone, keittiö, olohuone, makuuhuone, wc, koti | Tämä on keittiö., Olen kotona. | kotona as a fixed location form; tämä on | Reading, Speaking |
| a1.1-s4-l02 — Where is it? | Say where a familiar object is using a few fixed location patterns. | missä, on | pöytä, tuoli, sänky | Missä puhelin on?, Se on pöydällä., Se on huoneessa. | limited -ssa/-ssä and -lla/-llä location chunks | Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s4-l03 — Food and drink | Recognize and name a few very common foods and drinks. | — | kahvi, tee, maito, leipä, ruoka, vesi | Tämä on kahvia., Tämä on leipä. | mass/count distinction only through memorized examples | Listening, Reading, Speaking |
| a1.1-s4-l04 — I want … | Express one or two basic wants involving familiar food or drink. | — | nälkä, jano, haluan | Haluan kahvia., Haluan vettä., Minulla on nälkä. | partitive object after haluan as a phrase pattern; minulla on as a chunk | Listening, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s4-l05 — A very simple café order | Make a minimal polite order for one familiar item. | yksi | kahvila | Yksi kahvi, kiitos., Vettä, kiitos., Ole hyvä. | elliptical ordering phrases; recycling politeness | Listening, Reading, Speaking |
| a1.1-s4-l06 — Familiar animals 1 | Recognize and name five common animals. | — | koira, kissa, lintu, kala, hevonen | Tämä on koira., Se on kissa. | recycling demonstratives and olla | Listening, Reading, Speaking |

| a1.1-s4-l08 — Simple descriptions | Describe a familiar person, object or animal with one basic adjective. | — | punainen, valkoinen, musta, iso, pieni | Koira on iso., Kissa on pieni. | adjective predicative with olla | Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s4-l09 — My familiar world | Combine home, food, people and familiar objects in a few very short sentences. | — | — | Tämä on minun kotini., Keittiössä on …, Haluan …, Minulla on … | recycling only | Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing |
| a1.1-s4-l10 — A1.1 final checkpoint | Use memorized and highly practiced language across the core A1.1 situations. | — | — | — | final checkpoint: first contact, personal information, time/routine, home/basic needs | Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing |

## Data rules

- Inflected forms required for production tasks must be explicit in the lesson contract; the UI must not derive case forms by suffix concatenation.
- The language lesson explicitly supplies `suomea`, `persiaa`, and `englantia`.
- The personal-profile lesson uses complete forms such as `Olen Suomesta.` and `Asun Helsingissä.`.
- `prototype_mapping` and per-lesson `prototype_sources` must agree exactly.
